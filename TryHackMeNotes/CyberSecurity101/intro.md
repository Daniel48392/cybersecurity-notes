## What I learned:
  - Shodan - search engine of the internet of things, can search for anything with a network connection
  - VirusTotal - contains dozens of antivirus engines in one interface used to check websites and files
  - Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVE) - dictionary of known vulnerabilities
